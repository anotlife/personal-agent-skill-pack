# Citation Integrity

## Source rules

Prefer the original paper, official standard, official documentation, original dataset, or other primary material appropriate to the claim.

## Never invent

Do not guess:

- DOI;
- page numbers;
- issue/volume;
- author list;
- publication year;
- exact quotation;
- experimental detail not in the source.

## Citation scope

One citation should support the specific statement immediately before it. If a paragraph contains multiple claims from different sources, cite them separately.

## Literature synthesis

Do not turn a list of summaries into a literature review. Compare findings, methods, limitations, and disagreements. Make the synthesis explicit.
