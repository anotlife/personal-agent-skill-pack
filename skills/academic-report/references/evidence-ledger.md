# Evidence Ledger

For every important statement, assign one basis:

- **given** — directly supplied by the user;
- **observed** — measured or seen in the provided material;
- **derived** — computed from observations using a stated method;
- **source-backed** — supported by an external source;
- **interpretation** — explanatory reading of the evidence;
- **assumption** — temporary premise needed to proceed;
- **unknown** — not established.

## Rule

Never promote one category to another silently.

Examples:

- “The temperature was 80°C” must come from data or the user, not from a standard protocol.
- “The yield increased because of temperature” is an interpretation unless the design identifies causality.
- “The experiment proves…” is usually too strong for a single run.

## Minimal ledger columns

Claim | Basis | Source/data location | Confidence | What would falsify or weaken it?
