# Section Playbook

## Introduction / objective
State the question, context needed to understand it, and what will be tested or produced. Avoid broad historical filler.

## Method
Report what was actually done, not an idealized procedure that was intended but not followed.

## Results
Describe observations and calculated results before explaining them. Keep raw or representative evidence where appropriate.

## Discussion
Use the sequence:

**result → evidence → plausible explanation → alternative explanation → limitation → next check**

Do not repeat the Results section in decorative prose.

## Conclusion
Answer the stated question at the level justified by the evidence. Do not add new evidence here.

## Assignment adaptation
If the instructor provides headings, grading criteria, word limits, or a required format, those outrank this playbook.
