# Inference Failure Catalog

Look for:

- correlation presented as causation;
- small sample generalized to a broad population;
- selected examples treated as representative;
- survivorship / selection effects;
- base-rate neglect;
- false dichotomy;
- equivocation across meanings of a key term;
- scope jump from one context to another;
- anecdote treated as prevalence evidence;
- authority treated as proof when the authority's evidence is unspecified;
- “no evidence of X” treated as “evidence of no X”;
- unfalsifiable claims insulated from counterevidence.

## Repair

State what the evidence really establishes, narrow the claim, add the missing premise, or identify the missing evidence.
