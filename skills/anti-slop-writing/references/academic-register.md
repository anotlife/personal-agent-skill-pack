# Academic Register

Academic writing has valid conventions that should not be “de-AI'd” away.

Keep:

- domain terminology when it carries precise meaning;
- passive voice when the process matters more than the actor;
- formal structure required by the assignment;
- equations, units, statistical language, and methodological terms.

Remove:

- decorative academic filler;
- inflated claims;
- repetitive transitions;
- generic “significance” claims unsupported by the study.

Do not replace precise technical language with casual synonyms just to sound human.

Example principle:

“Samples were incubated at 37°C for 24 h” is already natural technical prose. Do not rewrite it merely because it sounds formal.
