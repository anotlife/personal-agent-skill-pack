# Voice and Specificity

Natural writing often contains concrete choices, not random quirks.

## Improve specificity by

- naming the actual object rather than “this aspect”;
- stating the real constraint rather than “various factors”;
- giving the exact number when verified;
- using the user's existing terminology consistently;
- deleting generic lead-ins.

## Personal voice

Use a person's style only from evidence they supplied: their previous writing, stated preferences, or explicit request. Do not infer personality from sparse context.

## Final test

A natural sentence can be plain. Do not optimize for “sounding human” at the expense of precision.
