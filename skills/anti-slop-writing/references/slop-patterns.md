# Slop Pattern Catalog

These are failure patterns, not a mandatory blacklist.

## Empty framing
Examples: generic openings, “it is important to note”, “in today's rapidly changing world”, generic significance paragraphs.

**Fix:** start with the actual subject, constraint, result, or question.

## Artificial contrast
Pattern: “This is not X; it is Y” used without a real conceptual contrast.

**Fix:** state the relationship directly.

## Forced triads
Everything becomes three parallel points merely for rhythm.

**Fix:** use as many points as the content requires.

## Significance inflation
A modest observation is framed as transformative, revolutionary, or broadly societal without evidence.

**Fix:** scale the claim back to the actual evidence.

## Synonym inflation
Simple words are replaced with grander but less precise words.

**Fix:** choose the most ordinary precise term.

## Formulaic closers
Paragraphs end with generic “therefore / broader implication” sentences that add no new information.

**Fix:** end where the reasoning ends.

## Fake specificity
Invented examples, statistics, labels, citations, or names added to make prose appear concrete.

**Fix:** use only supplied or verified specifics.

## Repetitive semantics
The same claim appears several times under different wording.

**Fix:** keep the strongest instance and remove echoes.
