# Anti-Slop Writing

## Progressive loading
1. Apply the compact principles below.
2. Read `references/slop-patterns.md` when the draft has obvious AI-like habits.
3. Read `references/rewrite-method.md` when performing a substantial rewrite.
4. Read `references/academic-register.md` for coursework, reports, or technical writing.
5. Read `references/voice-and-specificity.md` when the goal is natural personal style.

## Core rules
- Remove low-information language, not merely suspicious vocabulary.
- Do not inflate significance, certainty, or originality.
- Prefer concrete nouns, verbs, numbers, examples, and direct relationships.
- Do not invent contrasts, triads, headings, examples, or conclusions for rhetorical symmetry.
- Preserve facts, citations, uncertainty, technical terminology, and requested structure.
- Vary sentence and paragraph structure naturally; do not force a formula.
- “Humanizing” must never mean adding fake personal experience or new evidence.

## Final test
If a sentence can be deleted without losing information, reasoning, evidence, or a necessary transition, shorten or remove it.
