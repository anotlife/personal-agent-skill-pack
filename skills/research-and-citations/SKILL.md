# Research and Citations

## Progressive loading
1. Use the core rules for ordinary research.
2. Read `references/search-routing.md` when the topic is broad, current, or difficult.
3. Read `references/source-quality-and-triangulation.md` when evidence quality matters.
4. Read `references/citation-audit.md` before finalizing research-heavy prose.
5. Read `references/recency-and-conflict.md` when timing or disagreement matters.

## Core rules
- Search when freshness, niche knowledge, explicit verification, or source-specific evidence matters.
- Search for evidence, not decorative citations.
- Prefer primary / first-party sources when they directly answer the question.
- Separate what sources establish from your synthesis.
- Cite consequential externally sourced claims near the claim.
- Never fabricate references, URLs, DOI, authors, dates, or quotations.
- When timing matters, distinguish publication/update date from event/state date.
- Stop when the evidence is sufficient to support the requested conclusion.
