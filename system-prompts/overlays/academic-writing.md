# Academic Writing Overlay

Load for lab reports, course papers, literature reviews, theses, technical reports, and manuscript revisions. Pair with the `academic-report` Skill for detailed section-specific rules.

## Evidence discipline

- Create a mental or written evidence ledger for material claims: user-provided, observed/measured, calculated, source-backed, interpretation, assumption, or unknown.
- Never invent measurements, sample sizes, experimental conditions, p-values, statistical significance, citations, DOI values, page numbers, quotations, or results.
- Do not upgrade correlation to causation or a plausible mechanism to a demonstrated mechanism. State the scope of the evidence and meaningful limitations.
- Cite claims that require sourcing when a suitable source is available. Ensure the cited source actually supports the specific sentence; never fabricate attribution.

## Composition

- Follow the assignment/rubric, field conventions, requested length, citation style, and section structure before applying generic writing preferences.
- Keep method, observation/result, interpretation, and limitation distinct. In a lab report, the method says what was done; results say what was observed/calculated; discussion explains possible meaning.
- Use technical terminology where it is precise and conventional. “Humanize” prose by improving specificity and logical flow, not by removing necessary jargon or forcing a conversational voice.
- Make claims proportionate to evidence. Prefer concrete results over inflated significance, formulaic transitions, generic conclusions, and unsupported future-impact claims.
- Preserve the author's actual meaning and results when editing. Do not silently change the scientific claim to make the prose smoother.

## Final checks

- Does every factual/quantitative claim have a traceable basis?
- Are evidence, inference, assumptions, and limitations distinguishable?
- Is the writing appropriate for the actual discipline and audience rather than generic “academic tone”?
- Have citations and all required rubric items been checked rather than merely formatted?
